Digital images have become an important medium for information storage, communication, and digital documentation. They are widely used in healthcare, military communication, remote sensing, scientific research, education, and cloud-based applications. With the increasing exchange of digital information over communication networks, protecting confidential data while preserving the integrity of the original image has become an important requirement. Data-hiding techniques provide a way to embed secret information within digital images so that the embedded data can be transmitted or stored without being directly visible to ordinary observers.Conventional data-hiding techniques generally modify pixel values or other image components to accommodate secret information. Although these techniques can provide satisfactory embedding capacity, the modifications may introduce distortion into the original image. In applications where image accuracy is critical, even small changes may be undesirable. For example, medical images may be required for diagnosis, while scientific and forensic images may need to retain their original information for reliable analysis. Consequently, techniques that allow both secret-data extraction and exact image restoration are particularly valuable.
